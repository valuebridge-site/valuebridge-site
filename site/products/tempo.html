ValueBridge Tempo

Type a task the way you'd say it, press Auto-plan, and Tempo fits your work into the free time around your meetings. Tasks, scheduling, a focus timer, habits and reviews in one private app that opens in your web browser on a computer, tablet or phone. No account, no subscription, and your plans stay on your own device.

Who it's for

  • Freelancers, consultants and small-business owners juggling client work and admin
  • Managers and professionals who want time blocking without a subscription or a cloud account
  • Anyone who prefers their plans, habits and notes to stay on their own device

How it works

  1. Open the fileDouble-click ValueBridge_Tempo.html to open it in your web browser. On a phone, open it and add it to your home screen.
  2. Set your working dayChoose your hours, working days, lunch break and whether you focus best in the morning or the afternoon.
  3. Type your tasksWrite them as sentences, for example: “Send proposal tomorrow 3pm 45m !1 #client”. Tempo picks out the details.
  4. Auto-plan and focusPress Auto-plan to place tasks in your free time, then work through the blocks with the focus timer.

What's planned to be included

Today

Capacity meter, timeline, your three priorities, items that need attention, habits and your next alert.

Tasks

Quick add in plain sentences, projects, priorities 1 to 4, checklists, repeating tasks, and list or priority-matrix views.

Scheduler

Week view on a computer and day view on a phone. Drag blocks to move them in 15-minute steps. Auto-plan 7 days, with undo and a list of tasks that land after their due date.

Planning rules you set

Buffer around meetings, a protected lunch, a daily deep-work limit and a share of each day kept free for surprises.

Alerts

While Tempo is open: banners with a chime and snooze before meetings and task blocks, a morning briefing and an end-of-day reminder. Browser notifications where your browser allows them.

Focus timer

Timed work and break sessions linked to a task, with the time logged against it and a parking lot for stray thoughts.

Habits and insights

Weekly habit targets, streaks and 16 weeks of history. Focus trend, tasks completed, meeting load and how your estimates compare with the time you log.

Reviews

A daily shutdown checklist and a weekly review, with notes kept by date.

Your data

Backup and restore as copyable text, calendar export (.ics) for Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar, and task export as CSV.

Comfort

Dark mode, 12- or 24-hour clock, keyboard shortcuts and a command search (Ctrl K).

Requirements

A current web browser on a computer, tablet or phone, with permission to open a downloaded file. We test on real browsers and devices before release and will list the ones we have tested here.

Limits

  • No automatic sync between devices. Use the backup text to copy your data from one device to another.
  • Tempo cannot alert you when its tab is closed, because browsers stop a page's alerts then. Export to your calendar app for those reminders.
  • Calendar export only: no calendar import or two-way sync.
  • A personal planner: no team sharing or delegation.
  • Uses your device's time zone. English only.
  • Recurring events can be dragged to a new time, not a new day; open the event to change its day.

Questions

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account. Tempo is a file you open in your browser, and once it is open it runs without an internet connection. It does not upload your tasks, calendar or notes.

Is this a subscription?

No. It is a one-time licence, and minor updates within version 1 are free.

Will Tempo remind me when it is closed?

Not by itself: browsers stop a page's alerts when its tab is closed. Export your plan to Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar (.ics) and let that app send those reminders.

Can I use it on my phone and my computer?

Yes. The Standard licence covers up to 3 devices. Your data does not sync on its own; copy it between devices with the backup text in Settings.

What happens to my data if I clear my browser?

Clearing a browser's site data erases Tempo's data on that device. Keep a backup: Settings, Your data, Back up.

How is Tempo different from ValueBridge Dueline?

Tempo plans your days and weeks: tasks, time blocks and focus. Dueline keeps track of long-range dates such as renewals, expiry dates and birthdays.

Can I try it before I buy?

A free trial version with sample data is planned for this page. It will keep nothing after you close the tab and will leave out backup and export.
