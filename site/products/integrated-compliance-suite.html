Integrated Compliance Register Suite

Permits, legal requirements, environmental records, incidents and quality in one linked Excel workbook, plus changes, risks and opportunities and quality objectives. One Setup for up to 20 sites, one corrective action list with effectiveness checks, and one dashboard for the whole system.

Who it's for

  • Organisations running an integrated management system across ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001 topics
  • Compliance, quality and EHS leads who report one set of figures to management
  • Multi-site businesses that want one register system instead of a folder of separate trackers

How it works

  1. Look at the ExampleA fictional five-site group with each register and the dashboard filled in.
  2. Set up onceSites, countries, industries and thresholds feed all the registers.
  3. Start where it mattersPermits that expire first, then obligations and the legal register; incidents and quality records can start today.
  4. Run the monthly routineUpdate, clear the data checks, freeze the report date, print the Dashboard and log the trend.

What's planned to be included

Permits and licences

Lifecycle (renewable, one-time, to obtain, superseded) kept apart from applicability (confirmed, to verify). Lead time from the row, the regulator or criticality. Status, P1–P4 priority, renewal stage and an alert built from the status.

Obligations and calendar

Permit conditions and recurring duties (reports, fees, tests) with rolling due dates, and a 12-month renewal calendar.

Starter List and Library

170 starter pointers to check (81 permits, 42 legal duties, 47 recurring obligations) for Jamaica, Trinidad and Tobago, Barbados, The Bahamas, Cayman Islands, the United States (federal) and England, plus international good practice, across 12 industries. Each row shows the date it was checked. 64 regulators listed. Add your own rows and countries.

Legal register

What each requirement asks of you and how you meet it, by site or for all sites. Evaluation frequency, last evaluated and by whom, Met / Partly met / Not met, next evaluation and priority (ISO 14001 and 45001 cl. 6.1.3 and 9.1.2 topics).

Aspects and impacts

Severity x probability scoring with significance rules: an aspect linked to a legal requirement or a stakeholder concern counts as significant whatever its score.

Monitoring

Results against Max, Min or Range limits with Exceedance and Near limit flags, and a reminder to link an action to each exceedance.

Waste and duty of care

Tonnes from kg, t, L or m3; diversion rate; the carrier's licence checked against the collection date; hazardous waste manifests tracked.

Utilities and emissions estimates

Electricity, fuels and water by site and month, with Scope 1 and location-based Scope 2 estimates from factors you set.

Incidents and near misses

Actual and potential severity; the investigation level and due date follow from the potential. Root cause category and method, open actions and status.

Rates

TRIR (OSHA-style rate) and LTIFR (ILO-style rate) from hours worked by site and month. 'Possible recordable' is a screen, not a recording decision.

NCRs

Containment, disposition with concession approver, cost of poor quality, target close by severity, and a repeat flag for the same product within 12 months.

Audits and findings

Audit programme with method (on site, remote or hybrid) and auditor independence; findings graded with actions and verification (ISO 19011 topics).

Risks and opportunities

Scored and rated separately, with planned actions and review dates. References ISO 9001:2026 topics, including separate treatment of risks and opportunities.

Quality objectives

Target, direction, latest result, due date and status: On target, Off target or Missed.

Changes

Planned changes to processes, equipment, materials, people or suppliers, from approval to implementation and a review of results (ISO 9001 cl. 6.3 and ISO 45001 cl. 8.1.3 topics).

Quality Policy template

A one-page Word template with guidance, to adapt, sign and review.

Dashboard and trend

KPI cards, status by site, charts, critical items and the most overdue actions. A monthly trend log with a chart.

One action list

Correction, corrective, preventive and improvement actions with the source ID, owner, revised due dates and effectiveness checks (ISO cl. 10.2 topics).

Requirements

Microsoft Excel 2010 or newer, desktop, on Windows or Mac. No macros. Opens in Google Sheets and LibreOffice, where some drop-downs, colours and charts may differ. A Word-compatible app or PDF reader for the Playbook. Before release we open the files in desktop Excel and will list the Excel versions used here.

Limits

  • Excel only: no mobile app, photo capture, e-signatures or email reminders.
  • Up to 20 sites, with pre-built rows per register (for example 300 permits, 400 legal requirements, 400 incidents, 500 actions). Start Here explains how to extend them.
  • Starter pointers cover seven countries plus international good practice. They are not a full or up-to-date legal register, and there is no automatic legal-update service.
  • Statuses, scores and flags apply the rules and thresholds you set. They are a screen, not a legal determination, certification or audit opinion.
  • Clause pointers use the 2015 / 2018 numbering. The files reference standards only as topics and do not reproduce standard text.
  • The possible-recordable flag and investigation levels are simplified screens; your jurisdiction's rules decide what is recordable and reportable.
  • Emission factors are illustrative defaults: replace them. Scope 1 and 2 figures are self-prepared estimates, not a verified or assured inventory.

Questions

Can I buy the registers separately?

Yes. All five registers are also sold on their own, and the EHS Registers Suite combines the four EHS registers. This suite adds quality and puts everything in one workbook.

Is it written for ISO 9001:2026?

It references ISO 9001:2026 topics, including planning of changes and separate risks and opportunities, and keeps the 2015 clause numbers most readers still use. It does not reproduce any standard.

Which version of Excel do I need?

Microsoft Excel 2010 or newer on Windows or Mac. There are no macros. The files open in Google Sheets and LibreOffice, where some drop-downs, colours and charts may look different.

Does it make us compliant?

No. It organises your compliance information and applies the rules and thresholds you set. Statuses and flags are a screening aid, not a legal determination or an audit opinion.

Can a consultant use it with clients?

Not with this licence: it covers one organisation's own use. A Consultant licence is not offered yet.

Is it a subscription?

No. One-time licence, with minor updates within version 1 included.
