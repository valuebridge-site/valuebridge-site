Legal & Statutory Requirements Register

A legal register an auditor can test: each line says what the law asks of you, how you meet it and when you last evaluated it, with the next evaluation date, result and priority calculated. Start from starter pointers for your country and industry, and log changes in law as you hear of them.

Example workbook shown, rendered in LibreOffice; colours may differ slightly in Excel. Coralstone Group and all names, sites, permit numbers and figures are fictional.

Who it's for

  • Organisations working to ISO 14001, ISO 45001 or ISO 37301 topics that must show how they identify and evaluate compliance obligations
  • EHS and compliance managers who inherited a list of laws with no evaluation trail
  • Consultancies' in-house teams: one licence covers your own organisation

How it works

  1. Look at the ExampleSee a filled legal register with evaluations, results and actions.
  2. Pick your starter rowsOn Starter List choose a site, then check and copy the rows that apply.
  3. Write each line plainlyWhat it requires of us, how we meet it, the site and who evaluates it.
  4. Evaluate on a cycleRecord the date, result and evidence. Partly met or Not met lines link to an action.

What's planned to be included

Legal register

What each requirement asks of you and how you meet it, by site or for all sites. Evaluation frequency, last evaluated and by whom, Met / Partly met / Not met, next evaluation and priority (ISO 14001 and 45001 cl. 6.1.3 and 9.1.2 topics).

Change in Law

Log new or amended laws and guidance with the effective date, the sites affected and the legal register line it changes.

Starter List and Library

170 starter pointers to check (81 permits, 42 legal duties, 47 recurring obligations) for Jamaica, Trinidad and Tobago, Barbados, The Bahamas, Cayman Islands, the United States (federal) and England, plus international good practice, across 12 industries. Each row shows the date it was checked. 64 regulators listed. Add your own rows and countries.

Setup and sites

Organisation, report date (live, or frozen for month-end) and thresholds. Up to 20 sites, each with its own country and industry.

Dashboard and trend

KPI cards, status by site, charts, critical items and the most overdue actions. A monthly trend log with a chart.

One action list

Correction, corrective, preventive and improvement actions with the source ID, owner, revised due dates and effectiveness checks (ISO cl. 10.2 topics).

Data checks and rules

A data check column on each register names what is missing or inconsistent. The Reference sheet explains the rules the formulas follow.

Requirements

Microsoft Excel 2010 or newer, desktop, on Windows or Mac. No macros. Opens in Google Sheets and LibreOffice, where some drop-downs, colours and charts may differ. A Word-compatible app or PDF reader for the Playbook. Before release we open the files in desktop Excel and will list the Excel versions used here.

Limits

  • Excel only: no mobile app, photo capture, e-signatures or email reminders.
  • Up to 20 sites, with pre-built rows per register (for example 300 permits, 400 legal requirements, 400 incidents, 500 actions). Start Here explains how to extend them.
  • Starter pointers cover seven countries plus international good practice. They are not a full or up-to-date legal register, and there is no automatic legal-update service.
  • Statuses, scores and flags apply the rules and thresholds you set. They are a screen, not a legal determination, certification or audit opinion.
  • Clause pointers use the 2015 / 2018 numbering. The files reference standards only as topics and do not reproduce standard text.

Questions

Is the Library a full legal register for my country?

No. It is a set of starter pointers, checked as at the date shown on each row. Laws, thresholds and agency names change, so confirm each item with the authority or a qualified adviser.

Does it update when the law changes?

No. There is no automatic legal-update service. Use the Change in Law sheet to record changes you learn about, and the 'Change check' column to see lines nobody has checked recently.

Which version of Excel do I need?

Microsoft Excel 2010 or newer on Windows or Mac. There are no macros. The files open in Google Sheets and LibreOffice, where some drop-downs, colours and charts may look different.

Does it make us compliant?

No. It organises your compliance information and applies the rules and thresholds you set. Statuses and flags are a screening aid, not a legal determination or an audit opinion.

Can a consultant use it with clients?

Not with this licence: it covers one organisation's own use. A Consultant licence is not offered yet.

Is it a subscription?

No. One-time licence, with minor updates within version 1 included.
