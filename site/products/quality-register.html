Quality Register

The day-to-day records of a quality system in one Excel workbook: nonconformities, complaints, audits, documents, calibration, risks and opportunities, objectives and changes, all feeding one action list and one dashboard. It references ISO 9001:2026 topics, including planning of changes and separate treatment of risks and opportunities.

Screenshots

Example workbook shown, rendered in LibreOffice; colours may differ slightly in Excel. Coralstone Group and all names, sites, permit numbers and figures are fictional.

Who it's for

  • Quality managers in small and mid-sized businesses working to ISO 9001 topics
  • Organisations moving from ISO 9001:2015 to the 2026 edition that want to organise their evidence (2015 certificates are expected to move by September 2029)
  • Teams tracking complaints and nonconformities in scattered spreadsheets

How it works

  1. Look at the ExampleSee a repeat NCR, a concession, an overdue calibration and a high-rated risk.
  2. Set your targetsClose-out targets by severity, complaint response times and review cycles.
  3. Record as you goRaise NCRs and complaints, plan audits, keep documents and instruments current.
  4. Review monthlyCheck objectives off target, overdue items and actions awaiting an effectiveness check.

What's planned to be included

NCRs

Containment, disposition with concession approver, cost of poor quality, target close by severity, and a repeat flag for the same product within 12 months.

Complaints

Acknowledge-by and resolve-by targets, justified or not, escalation, and the date the outcome was told to the customer (ISO 10002 topics).

Audits and findings

Audit programme with method (on site, remote or hybrid) and auditor independence; findings graded with actions and verification (ISO 19011 topics).

Documents and records

Revision, owner, approver, review cycle, retention period and disposal date.

Calibration

Intervals, next due, 'Overdue – do not use', accredited provider, and an impact check after a failed calibration.

Risks and opportunities

Scored and rated separately, with planned actions and review dates. References ISO 9001:2026 topics, including separate treatment of risks and opportunities.

Quality objectives

Target, direction, latest result, due date and status: On target, Off target or Missed.

Changes

Planned changes to processes, equipment, materials, people or suppliers, from approval to implementation and a review of results (ISO 9001 cl. 6.3 and ISO 45001 cl. 8.1.3 topics).

Quality Policy template

A one-page Word template with guidance, to adapt, sign and review.

Dashboard and trend

KPI cards, status by site, charts, critical items and the most overdue actions. A monthly trend log with a chart.

One action list

Correction, corrective, preventive and improvement actions with the source ID, owner, revised due dates and effectiveness checks (ISO cl. 10.2 topics).

Data checks and rules

A data check column on each register names what is missing or inconsistent. The Reference sheet explains the rules the formulas follow.

Requirements

Microsoft Excel 2010 or newer, desktop, on Windows or Mac. No macros. Opens in Google Sheets and LibreOffice, where some drop-downs, colours and charts may differ. A Word-compatible app or PDF reader for the Playbook. Before release we open the files in desktop Excel and will list the Excel versions used here.

Limits

  • Excel only: no mobile app, photo capture, e-signatures or email reminders.
  • Up to 20 sites, with pre-built rows per register (for example 300 permits, 400 legal requirements, 400 incidents, 500 actions). Start Here explains how to extend them.
  • Starter pointers cover seven countries plus international good practice. They are not a full or up-to-date legal register, and there is no automatic legal-update service.
  • Statuses, scores and flags apply the rules and thresholds you set. They are a screen, not a legal determination, certification or audit opinion.
  • Clause pointers use the 2015 / 2018 numbering. The files reference standards only as topics and do not reproduce standard text.

Questions

Does using it lead to certification?

No product can promise that. The register organises your records and evidence; certification depends on your system and an accredited certification body's audit.

Is it written for ISO 9001:2026?

It references ISO 9001:2026 topics, including planning of changes and separate risks and opportunities, and keeps the 2015 clause numbers most readers still use. It does not reproduce the standard.

Which version of Excel do I need?

Microsoft Excel 2010 or newer on Windows or Mac. There are no macros. The files open in Google Sheets and LibreOffice, where some drop-downs, colours and charts may look different.

Does it make us compliant?

No. It organises your compliance information and applies the rules and thresholds you set. Statuses and flags are a screening aid, not a legal determination or an audit opinion.

Can a consultant use it with clients?

Not with this licence: it covers one organisation's own use. A Consultant licence is not offered yet.

Is it a subscription?

No. One-time licence, with minor updates within version 1 included.
