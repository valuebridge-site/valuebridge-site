About ValueBridge

ValueBridge makes practical digital tools: spreadsheet templates, document kits and small software apps for small businesses, professionals and households.

We are a Jamaican business. Our products come from hands-on experience in quality, compliance, safety, environmental management and business administration, and they are designed for the way small teams actually work: offline-friendly, buy once where possible, and with worked examples so you can see a tool working before you start.

How we release products

Nothing goes on sale until it has passed our review process. Before release, each product is tested on real computers and phones, then reviewed by real users or, for safety, finance, environmental and engineering products, by a qualified specialist. Licence and product wording are checked by an attorney. That is why every product is marked Coming soon today.
